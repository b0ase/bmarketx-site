Privacy

bMarketX.com is run by The Bitcoin Corporation Ltd. Last updated 27 October 2026. This page covers the bMarketX website. Your wallet is covered by the bWalletX privacy policy.

In short

Your bWalletX is your account: we don't ask for your name, email or a password. We keep what a marketplace needs to work (stores, listings, orders and the address you post to), we count visits without cookies so we know which campaigns work, and we never sell your data, share it with advertisers or follow you around other sites.

What we keep

  • Your wallet’s public identity key, when you connect bWalletX. It is your account. A signed cookie keeps you signed in on this browser for up to 7 days.
  • Stores and listings you create or bring from eBay: names, prices, photos, descriptions and stock. These are public.
  • Orders: the listing, price, the BSV payment and its transaction, the buyer’s and seller’s identity keys, and the postal address you enter at checkout. Only you and that seller can see the address. We keep orders as your record of the sale.
  • eBay, if you sign in with eBay: your eBay user ID, username or store name, and your positive-feedback percentage, to verify your store. eBay’s sign-in token is kept for at most 2 hours in a locked cookie on your browser, only to bring your listings across, and never in our database. If you close your eBay account, eBay tells us and we delete what we hold from eBay about you. Linking eBay inside bWalletX stores nothing here.
  • Feedback you send with the “Give feedback” button: the score, your comment, and your email or wallet key only if you choose to add them.
  • Abuse limits: a short-lived counter per wallet or IP address, so nobody can flood the site. Deleted within a day.

Visit counts and campaigns

We count page views and where visits come from (the referring site, and campaign tags such as ?utm_campaign=… on our links) using Vercel Web Analytics, which sets no cookies and doesn’t identify you. We also count a few steps that tell us bMarketX is working: a wallet connected, a store opened, listings brought in, a sale. Each is recorded with the campaign that brought the visit, if any, not with your name, address or IP address. We use these counts only to improve bMarketX and decide where to advertise. There are no advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers and no cross-site tracking.

Ads on bMarketX

Some pages show square bAds down the side, served by our own ad server at bChatX (also The Bitcoin Corporation Ltd). It counts how often each ad is seen and clicked, without cookies. Ads are never targeted at you personally.

Kept in your browser only

Your Watchlist, Recently viewed, the postal address you last used at checkout and the campaign that first brought you here are stored in your browser (localStorage). We can’t see them. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Services we use

  • Vercel hosts the site and runs the visit counts.
  • Our own database on Hetzner servers holds stores, listings and orders.
  • WhatsOnChain: the live BSV price, and checking that payments reached the network.
  • eBay: Sign in with eBay, your listings and photos (photos load from eBay’s image servers).
  • Google Fonts, unpkg and cdnjs deliver fonts and scripts to your browser, which shares your IP address with them.

Payments are public

Payments go straight from your wallet to the seller’s on the BSV blockchain, which is public. The transaction is visible to anyone; your name and address are not in it.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you by writing to support@bwalletx.com. We will remove your stores, listings and eBay details; we keep order records where the law requires us to keep records of sales. Under UK and EU law you can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).
